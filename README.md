## 🎵 Spotify-Style Mini Player

Fixed at the bottom of the website, styled with luxury dark glassmorphism:

### 🎧 Playlist:
1. **Rab Wangu** — Jass Manak (`public/music/rab-wangu.mp3`)
2. **Noormahal** (`public/music/noormahal.mp3`)

### ✨ Player Features:
- **Instant Autoplay**: Starts playing *Rab Wangu* immediately on website open. If blocked by the browser, it starts on the first click/tap anywhere.
- **Continuous Playback**: Music never restarts during re-renders or section navigation.
- **Auto Next-Song**: When *Rab Wangu* finishes, it automatically transitions smoothly to *Noormahal*, and loops seamlessly.
- **Grabbable & Drag-to-Seek Progress Bar**:
  - Drag the progress dot (●) forward or backward to ANY position in the song with mouse or touch.
  - Click anywhere on the progress bar to jump there instantly.
  - Live elapsed time & total duration indicators.
- **Playback Controls**: Previous, Play/Pause, Next, Repeat Toggle, Playlist Drawer, Volume Slider & Mute.
